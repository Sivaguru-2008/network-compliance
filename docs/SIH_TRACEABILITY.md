## Architectural Guarantees

1. **Security Rule (No AI Compliance Autonomy)**: AI models propose *candidate semantic interpretations* with confidence scores; AI models **never** output `PASS` or `FAIL`. All compliance verdicts are strictly determined by deterministic boolean/ternary logic rules.
2. **Offline-First Resilience**: If an LLM is unavailable or unconfigured, the system defaults to deterministic keyword-based heuristics or marks unrecognized syntax as `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
3. **Data Provenance Preservation**: All synthetic fixtures and official vendor examples are explicitly labelled; no synthetic data is ever misrepresented as real-device capture.
